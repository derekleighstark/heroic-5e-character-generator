Player Reference

Core Rolls

Skill / Save: 1d20 + ability modifier + Prowess if trained.

Attack: 1d20 + delivery ability + Prowess. Power EV: 10 + governing ability + Prowess.

Strong success: beat EV by 5+; one Momentum Effect. Heroic: beat by 10+; one Heroic Effect or two Momentum Effects.

Natural 20: combat attack critical. Natural 1: attack misses; failed Active Defense lets attacker critical.

HP: Level 1 = CON score + maximum Hit Die × Rank HP multiplier; later levels add die median + CON modifier. Tough adds +2 HP/level.

Range Bands

Close: same zone, 0–25 ft.

Short: adjacent zone, 25–50 ft.

Medium: 2–3 zones, 50–120 ft.

Long: 4–6 zones, 120–300 ft.

Turn Economy

Move, Action, Bonus Action when granted, Reaction when triggered, one free object interaction, and speech.

Edge — Spend After the Roll, Before the Outcome; One Use per Roll

1 Reroll d201 Advantage / cancel Disadvantage1 Heroic Defense1 Dramatic Rebound1 Resist Defeat (1/encounter)1 Power Stunt1 Add 1 Power Die1 Reduce Collateral1 Narrative Advantage2 Refresh Encounter (1/encounter)2 Push Daily; +1 Burnout1 each Team Combo

Starting Edge: Street 3, Mid 2, World 1. Cap = Level + Prowess. Starting Edge refreshes each session; refresh does not fill to cap.

Recovery

Breather (5–10 min): spend any Hit Dice. Each heals die + CON + Recovery Mod, minimum die median; refresh Breather abilities.

Second Wind: Bonus Action once/encounter; spend 1 Hit Die; end Shaken, Slowed, or Dazed; Advantage on next save before end of next turn.

Steady On: while Bloodied, Action once/encounter; spend 1 Hit Die + Recovery Mod.

Downtime: full HP and Hit Dice, starting Edge, Daily powers, and most Conditions.

Downed & Grit

At 0 HP you are Downed. Make Grit checks as directed. Three successes stabilize at 0 HP, unconscious. Three failures mean Defeated. Medicine DC 10 stabilizes.

Sustained Powers

Use a Bonus Action at the start of your turn. You may sustain only one power at a time. Sustaining ends if Stunned, Incapacitated, or Unconscious.

Burnout

Check: 1d20 + CON + Prowess vs DC 10 + (current Burnout × 2). After safely maintaining a Power for Prowess turns, check each extra turn.

Condition Reference

Condition Reference

Large-print table for faster at-the-table use.

BlindedAttacks against gain Adv.; own attacks and sight checks have Disadv.

BurningSource Prowess fire at turn start; Action + reasonable method removes.

CharmedCannot willingly attack source; Disadv. on saves against source.

DazedEither Action or Bonus Action; no Reactions.

DeafenedCannot hear; hearing-required effects fail.

FrightenedDisadv. while source visible; cannot willingly move closer.

FrozenCannot move; Disadv. Dodge; Restrained; end-turn STR/CON save.

GrappledSpeed 0; Action Athletics/Acrobatics vs grappler Athletics to escape.

HiddenAgainst Disadv.; own attacks Adv.; ends when revealed/attacking/visible Power.

ImmobilizedCannot move by any means; no Athletics escape.

IncapacitatedNo actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, or movement; usually until Breather.

InjuredDisadv. one skill/physical save; Medicine DC 15 during Breather/Downtime.

OverloadedDisadv. next Power or Technology roll; ends after that roll.

ParalyzedNo movement/actions; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks gain Adv.

PinnedGrappled and Prone; escaping grapple ends both.

PoisonedDisadvantage on attacks and Skill checks.

Power DisruptedNamed set suppressed; passives inactive; Sustained effects end.

ProneMelee against Adv.; ranged against Disadv.; stand for half Speed.

RestrainedSpeed 0; attacks/Dodge Disadv.; attacks against gain Adv.

ShakenDisadv. next attack, save, or Skill check, then ends.

SlowedHalf Speed; no Reactions; either Action or Bonus Action.

StunnedNo movement/Action/Bonus Action; Active Defenses have Disadv.

UnconsciousCannot act/move; Prone; auto-fail STR/DEX; attacks Adv.; Close hits critical.
